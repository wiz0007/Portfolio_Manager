# Investment Knowledge Base — Research and Verification Assignment

## Objective

Expand and verify an existing Investment Knowledge Base for an Indian multi-asset portfolio management platform.

The research must cover three areas:

1. Indian banking products: savings accounts, fixed deposits, and auto-sweep facilities.
2. Real estate valuation and transaction costs in Uttarakhand.
3. Real estate valuation and transaction costs in Delhi (NCT).

The final research will be used for future database design, financial calculations, API integrations, investment comparisons, and historical portfolio valuation.

This is a technical research assignment. Do not produce a generic financial education article.

## 1. Research and source requirements

- Prioritize official bank websites, bank product documents, government notifications, and official registration portals.
- Use secondary sources only when official information is unavailable.
- Provide direct URLs for every important finding.
- Record effective dates, publication dates, and research/retrieval dates.
- Distinguish current information from outdated information.
- Never invent missing rates, thresholds, fees, or eligibility conditions.
- Mark unverifiable information as "Not publicly verified".
- Preserve conflicting official findings and explain the discrepancy.
- Distinguish bank-wide rules from account-specific product rules.
- Distinguish government circle rates from actual property market prices.
- Use effective-dated records rather than treating rates as permanent values.

Use the latest information available at the time of research. State the actual research date.

## 2.1 Savings account interest rates
For every bank, collect:
- Current interest rate.
- Balance slabs and thresholds.
- Whether rates are progressive/incremental or applied to the entire balance.
- Interest calculation method.
- Interest credit frequency.
- Account-specific eligibility conditions.
- Senior citizen or special customer variations, where applicable.
- Effective date.
- Official source URL.

## 2.2 Fixed deposit interest rates
For every bank, collect:
- Tenure-wise interest rates.
- Minimum and maximum deposit tenure.
- Callable versus non-callable FD differences.
- Senior citizen additional interest.
- Premature withdrawal conditions and penalties.
- Minimum deposit amount, where applicable.
- Tax-saver FD availability, lock-in, and rates.
- Auto-renewal conditions.
- Effective date and source.

## 2.3 Auto-sweep / sweep-in / sweep-out facilities
Investigate the availability and exact terms of auto-sweep facilities for every bank.

For each product or account variant, collect:
- Bank name.
- Exact product/account variant.
- Eligibility conditions.
- Sweep-out trigger balance.
- Minimum retained savings balance.
- Minimum FD creation amount.
- Sweep-out increment.
- Sweep-in trigger balance.
- Sweep-in increment.
- Linked FD tenure.
- Applicable FD interest rate.
- FIFO or LIFO withdrawal order.
- Whether customers can select withdrawal order.
- Whether principal alone or principal plus interest is considered.
- Premature withdrawal penalties.
- Minimum FD holding period.
- Senior citizen rate applicability.
- Auto-renewal conditions.
- Product exclusions and restrictions.
- Effective date.
- Source URL/document.
